I contributed to multiway point cloud registration for the Nothing Stands Still Challenge. Team Curaytor placed first at CVPR 2026, following the Urban Robotics Lab team's first place at ICRA 2025. The challenge evaluates alignment across scans of construction scenes that change over time.
