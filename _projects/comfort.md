Our team placed first overall among 41 teams at the IROS 2026 COMFORT Localization Challenge. The system fuses two LiDARs, an IMU, and a camera. Our technical report reports a mean absolute trajectory error (ATE) of 1.40 cm across six GrandTour test scenes using one configuration.

[Technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22047) · [Code and run videos](https://github.com/url-kaist/se3-livom-comfort) · [Lab announcement](https://urobot.kaist.ac.kr/announcement/?mod=document&uid=655)
