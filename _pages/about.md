I am a PhD candidate in the [Urban Robotics Lab](https://urobot.kaist.ac.kr/url_teams/mr-seungjae-lee/) at KAIST, advised by Prof. Hyun Myung. I work on 3D point cloud registration and LiDAR SLAM, with a focus on reliable localization and mapping when the environment changes.

My work spans multiway point cloud registration, LiDAR perception, and localization for autonomous quadrupeds. See [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) for selected work or [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) for education, industry experience, and awards.
